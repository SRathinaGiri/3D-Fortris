    How to play

    • Pieces fall inside a 3D well. Fill every cell of a horizontal layer to clear it: 100 points × your level per layer.
    • A new piece waits at the top (⏸ HOLD on the bar) so you can line it up. Press DROP (Space) to release it, and DROP again to slam it down.
    • The ghost and the floor markers show where the piece will land; the thin lines drop from each column.
    • Arrows follow the camera: ▲ always moves the piece away from you. Turn the view with ↶ ↷ (V), look straight down with ⊡ (T), or drag to orbit.
    • 💣 Bomb: blows away the block it lands on (+50).
    • NEXT (right) shows the coming three pieces as 3D models; LAYERS (left) counts the blocks the bottom layers still need.
    • A new level every 5 layers: pieces fall faster and score more; the well carries on as it is. A new game always starts at level 1; your game is saved and Continue picks it up later.
    • Height (menu): 15 by default, 12 for small screens, 20 = Classic. A lower well shows bigger in side-by-side 3D on a phone.

    3D viewing modes

    • Parallel 3D: left image for left eye — cardboard viewers or parallel free-viewing.
    • Cross-Eye 3D: cross your eyes until a third image fuses in the middle.
    • Anaglyph: red/cyan glasses.
    • Smart Glasses (HSBS): 16:9 half side-by-side for RayNeo Air, XREAL, Rokid, TCL — switch the glasses to 3D SBS mode.
    • 🥽 VR: appears in the Meta Quest browser. The well stands in front of you; thumbstick moves, triggers / grips rotate, A drops, B pauses.
    • 3D Depth scales with the camera distance; lower it if your eyes strain. Focus moves the screen plane.
